# Design Science – Engineering Mature Technologies

## Purpose

TaylorDo is based on the premise that mature technologies continue to deserve thoughtful engineering while they remain in widespread operational use.

Innovation is often associated with new technologies. However, mature technologies continue to support millions of systems in homes, businesses, educational institutions, and governments throughout the world.

Mechanical hard disk drives remain an important component of computing infrastructure because they continue to provide large storage capacities at low cost and remain well suited to archival, development, backup, and general-purpose storage.

As long as these systems remain in active service, opportunities continue to exist for improving their efficiency, reliability, maintainability, and operational lifetime.

TaylorDo approaches hard disk optimization from this perspective.

The objective is not to preserve older technology for its own sake, but to apply sound engineering principles to systems that continue to provide practical value.

Good engineering does not end when a technology matures.

It continues for as long as the technology remains useful.
