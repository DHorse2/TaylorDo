# HDD Market

The HDD market has split into two distinct segments.

Consumer HDD use has declined as SSD adoption has increased.

Enterprise and nearline HDD capacity demand continues because cloud storage, AI, backups, archives, and large datasets require vast amounts of economical storage.

Manufacturers may ship fewer drives while shipping far more total capacity.

This means TaylorDo has two distinct relevance paths:

1. Practical Windows HDD support for people and organizations still using existing HDD-based systems.
2. Design Science and storage architecture research relevant to large-scale storage thinking.

Future versions of this document should include cited market data and projections.
