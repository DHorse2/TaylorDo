# Environmental Considerations

TaylorDo's environmental argument is based on the aggregate value of extending the useful life of existing computer systems.

Where improved maintenance delays replacement, reduces unnecessary drive activity, or improves usefulness of existing systems, it may contribute to reduced electronic waste and more responsible use of computing resources.

These claims should be framed carefully.

TaylorDo should avoid claiming guaranteed environmental impact without measurement.

The strongest position is that better use of existing hardware can be environmentally relevant when applied across large numbers of systems.
