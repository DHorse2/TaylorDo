# Modern HDDs

Modern HDDs are not simply larger versions of earlier drives.

They include:

- Higher areal density.
- Zoned Bit Recording.
- Large onboard cache.
- Native Command Queuing.
- Sophisticated firmware.
- Advanced error correction.
- Helium-filled enterprise designs.
- CMR and SMR recording technologies.

Despite these advances, modern HDDs remain mechanical devices.

Their intelligence has increased substantially.

Their physics have not.

This makes workload-oriented organization, locality of reference, and reduction of cumulative seek activity still relevant.
