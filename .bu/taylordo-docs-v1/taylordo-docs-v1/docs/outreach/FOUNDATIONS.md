# Foundations and Public Benefit Organizations

Foundations and public benefit organizations may be interested in TaylorDo where it intersects with:

- Digital inclusion.
- Sustainability.
- Extending the life of existing computers.
- Support for schools and community organizations.
- Reduction of electronic waste.

The outreach posture should not be a funding request.

It should be an invitation to evaluate, share, or consider TaylorDo as an open resource where it aligns with existing programs.
