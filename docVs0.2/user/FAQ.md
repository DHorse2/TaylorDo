# FAQ

## Is TaylorDo a PC speed booster?

No. TaylorDo should not be marketed as a generic PC speed booster.

It is an HDD organization and maintenance tool.

It may significantly improve performance on highly fragmented and nearly full HDD systems, but well-maintained systems may show only modest improvement.

## Does TaylorDo help SSDs?

TaylorDo is designed for HDD optimization and should avoid unnecessary SSD defragmentation.

## Why does file placement matter?

Mechanical HDDs have moving heads. The physical location of related data affects seek time and cumulative access cost.
