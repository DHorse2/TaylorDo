# Outreach Philosophy

TaylorDo seeks engagement before endorsement.

The project is offered as an open engineering effort whose ideas, implementation, and documentation are intended to be examined, discussed, tested, and improved.

Outreach is therefore based upon:

- Sharing knowledge.
- Inviting technical evaluation.
- Encouraging collaboration.
- Supporting education.
- Promoting responsible use of existing computing resources.

Organizations are not approached as sponsors or funding sources.

They are approached as potential collaborators, reviewers, educators, practitioners, and members of a broader engineering community.

The objective is to contribute useful ideas and practical tools that others may evaluate on their own merits.

## Outreach Audiences

TaylorDo may be relevant to:

- Users maintaining existing HDD-based computers.
- Universities and computer science departments.
- Government ministries responsible for technology or digital inclusion.
- Chambers of commerce and small-business communities.
- Enterprises and data centers willing to evaluate storage organization concepts.
- Foundations and NGOs interested in digital inclusion and sustainability.
