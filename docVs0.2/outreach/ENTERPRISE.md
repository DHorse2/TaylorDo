# Enterprise Evaluation

Enterprise outreach is primarily about validation.

The goal is not immediate adoption.

The goal is to find technically capable organizations willing to evaluate the underlying storage organization model.

Possible evaluation questions include:

- Does workload-oriented organization measurably reduce seek activity?
- Does locality of reference improve sustained workloads?
- Which workloads benefit most?
- What can TaylorDo learn from modern large-scale storage environments?

A single serious enterprise or data-center evaluation could provide substantial technical credibility.
