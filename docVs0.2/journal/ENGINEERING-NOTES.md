# Engineering Notes

## Notes Captured

- Highly fragmented and nearly full HDDs may experience significant or dramatic performance restoration after optimization.
- Well-maintained HDDs may only show modest improvements.
- TaylorDo's value is not merely defragmentation but optimized placement of performance-impacting data.
- Application executables, libraries, configuration, and related data should be stored near one another where practical.
- Developer files and gaming workloads are important examples of workload-oriented placement.
- Documentation should distinguish engineering claims from aspirational benefits.
- Environmental claims should be framed as potential aggregate benefits pending measurement.
