# Jeroen Kessels

Jeroen Kessels is acknowledged as the creator of JkDefrag and MyDefrag.

His work demonstrated that disk optimization could be approached through script-driven organization, progressive passes, and workload-aware placement.

TaylorDo builds upon those ideas by extending storage classification, broadening workload-oriented organization, modernizing the supporting tooling, and documenting the engineering principles that underlie the system.

This document is reserved for a fuller history of Jeroen Kessels, JkDefrag, MyDefrag, and the technical lineage of TaylorDo.
