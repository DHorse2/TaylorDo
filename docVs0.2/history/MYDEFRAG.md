# MyDefrag

MyDefrag provided the engine and scripting model upon which TaylorDo was initially built.

MyDefrag's important design contribution was the recognition that optimization could involve repeated passes through a disk, selecting files matching specific categories and progressively creating storage zones.

This enabled storage organization rather than simple defragmentation.

TaylorDo extends this concept through a more complete classification of the contents of a Windows system and a generalized workload-oriented storage architecture.
