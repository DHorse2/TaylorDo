# TaylorDo

TaylorDo began as a MyDefrag-based optimization project and evolved into a broader open engineering project.

Its major themes include:

- Workload-oriented storage architecture.
- Fine-grained classification of Windows systems.
- Preventive maintenance.
- Scheduled automation.
- SSD safety.
- Documentation of Design Science principles.
- Future engine independence.

TaylorDo is intended to provide a practical easy-install tool while also preserving and extending knowledge about mechanical hard disk optimization.
