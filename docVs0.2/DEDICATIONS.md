# Dedications

TaylorDo is dedicated to two individuals whose contributions shaped the project in different ways.

## Dedication

TaylorDo is dedicated to **Matt Taylor** in recognition of his influence on the broader vision and philosophy that inspired this project.

This dedication reflects the origins of the project and remains an integral part of its history.

Additional historical context may be maintained in [history/MATT-TAYLOR.md](history/MATT-TAYLOR.md).

## Acknowledgement

TaylorDo also respectfully acknowledges **Jeroen Kessels**, creator of **JkDefrag** and **MyDefrag**.

His pioneering work demonstrated that disk optimization could be approached as a disciplined engineering problem through script-driven optimization, progressive storage organization, and workload-oriented design.

TaylorDo builds upon these ideas by extending the storage architecture, expanding file classification, developing a generalized workload-oriented organization model, modernizing the supporting tooling, and documenting the engineering principles that underlie the system.

This project is offered with appreciation for his significant contribution to the field of storage optimization.

Additional historical context may be maintained in [history/JEROEN-KESSELS.md](history/JEROEN-KESSELS.md).
