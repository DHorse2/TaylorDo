# Database

TaylorDo outreach planning includes a MySQL database for maintaining contacts and users.

The database should support:

- Contact management.
- Consent and opt-out tracking.
- Outreach history.
- Segmentation.
- Target categories such as universities, government ministries, chambers of commerce, HDD forums, enterprises, and foundations.

Email campaigns must avoid spam classification and should respect consent, relevance, and unsubscribe expectations.
